# Outreach email – website proposal (English)

Replace everything in `[brackets]`. The more specific line in the **[Personal hook]** is what gets replies, so write one per venue (examples below).

---

**Subject options** (pick one)
- A website for [Café Name]? I made you a free preview
- [Café Name] on Google: a quick idea
- Loved [signature item] at [Café Name], one idea for you

---

Hi [Owner / Manager name, or "[Café Name] team"],

My name is Khaled, and I'm a web designer in Doha. [Personal hook, e.g. "I came across [Café Name] on Gewan Island last week, and your [signature dish/drink] is all over my feed."]

I noticed you don't have a website yet. Your Instagram looks great, but a lot of people who hear about you never see it. Here's what a simple website would add:

- **Found on Google, not just Instagram.** When someone searches "cafe Gewan Island" or "brunch near me", Google shows businesses that have a website. Without one, the traffic goes to the café next door.
- **Your menu, always up to date.** No more screenshots in DMs or old prices on delivery apps. Guests see your real menu, photos and hours on their phone before they arrive.
- **One tap to book, order or WhatsApp you.** Table bookings, cake and catering orders, and directions on Google Maps all in one place. You get the full order, and no app takes a commission.
- **Credibility with tourists, companies and event planners.** Visitors, corporate clients and wedding or event planners check for a website before they call. A page with your own domain (e.g. [cafename].qa) looks established.
- **You own it.** Instagram's algorithm can change, and an account can be restricted overnight. Your website and the customers it brings stay yours.

**What I'd like to offer:** I'll design a **free homepage mockup for [Café Name]**, using your branding, photos and menu, so you can see exactly what it would look like before you decide anything. No cost and no obligation.

Here's an example I built for a Doha café: [link to Jasper Café demo]. It's bilingual, fast on mobile, and sends cake orders straight to WhatsApp.

Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call or coffee this week? Or just reply "yes" and I'll send the mockup within 3 days.

Best regards,
**Khaled**
Web Designer – Doha
[WhatsApp number] · [email] · [portfolio link]

*If you'd rather not hear from me again, just reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.*

## Short WhatsApp / Instagram DM version

Most leads only list a phone number or Instagram. For a first contact there, use this shorter message:

> Hi [Café Name] team 👋 I'm Khaled, a web designer here in Doha. I love what you've built at [location]. I noticed you don't have a website yet, so people searching Google for cafés nearby can't find you. I'd be happy to design a **free homepage mockup** for you, with no cost or obligation. Can I send it over? Example of my work: [Jasper demo link]

## Follow-up (4–5 days later, only once)

> Hi [Name], just following up on my note about a website for [Café Name]. The free mockup offer still stands, and I can have it ready in 3 days. Should I go ahead?
